# German B1 curriculum audit

## Decision

B1 is structurally complete but is not yet a self-contained strong-B1 course. It has 14 ordered chapters, 42 lessons, 318 reusable sentence cards, 42 grammar topics, and 336 lesson questions. A content-integrity audit quarantined 876 synthetic vocabulary combinations. Reviewed replacement batch one raises the published B1 bank to 351 items; rebuilding continues.

The app marks a learner ready for B2 study only after every B1 lesson is complete, every lesson quiz is at least 80%, the seven-skill final is at least 80% overall with at least 70% in each skill, and no review is due. Writing and speaking finals now use tolerant, word-order-aware model matching instead of brittle exact-string grading. They remain controlled-production checks, not human ratings of free writing, conversation, or pronunciation. Native-speaker approval and real learner evidence remain external release gates.

## Remaining external gates

- A real native German reviewer must approve the complete B1 book.
- Recorded listening should replace device text-to-speech for a consistent voice, natural rhythm, and speaker variety.
- A qualified evaluator or validated assessment service must rate extended free writing, interaction, and pronunciation against an explicit B1 rubric.
- Real learners must test outcomes and usability; automated tests cannot replace this evidence.
- Outcome evidence should include an external CEFR-aligned benchmark, not only completion and in-app scores.

## Outcome-hardening order

1. Complete native-speaker review of A1, A2, and B1 language, sequencing, and cultural naturalness.
2. Add human-recorded German listening sets with more than one speaker and normal-speed connected speech.
3. Add authentic B1 portfolio tasks: a connected message, an opinion text, a short presentation, and an interactive role-play.
4. Score the portfolio with a B1 rubric covering task completion, range, accuracy, coherence, interaction, intelligibility, and repair strategies.
5. Run the documented learner pilot and compare results with an external CEFR-aligned assessment before making a broad outcome claim.
