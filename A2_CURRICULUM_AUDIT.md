# German A2 curriculum audit

## Decision

The A2 curriculum is structurally complete but is not yet sufficient as the only route to B1. It has 14 ordered chapters, 42 lessons, 318 reusable sentence cards, 42 grammar topics, and 336 lesson questions. A content-integrity audit quarantined 876 synthetic vocabulary combinations. Reviewed replacement batch one raises the published A2 bank to 352 items; rebuilding continues.

The app recommends B1 only when a learner completes every A2 lesson, scores at least 80% on every lesson quiz and final overall, reaches at least 70% in all seven final-assessment skills, and clears due review. Native-speaker approval and real learner evidence remain external gates.

## Sequence

1. Everyday connections
2. A practical day
3. Experiences and stories
4. Home and community
5. Work and study
6. Shopping and services
7. Travel and transport
8. Health and wellbeing
9. Relationships and events
10. Media and technology
11. Grammar connections
12. Plans and choices
13. Integrated communication
14. A2 review and production

## Coverage

- Connected past narration with Perfekt, war, hatte, and past modal forms
- Reasons, reported content, indirect questions, conditions, and contrast
- Accusative/dative control, two-way prepositions, relative clauses, and adjective endings
- Housing, neighbors, work, study, returns, banking, appointments, travel problems, accommodation, health, relationships, technology, and media
- Longer reading and listening strategies
- Structured informal and formal writing
- Connected speaking, repair language, polite negotiation, and real-life problem solving
- Searchable audio-enabled published vocabulary with saved review; the supplemental bank remains below target during the quality rebuild
- Seven-skill final and persisted readiness decision

## Remaining evidence

- A real native German reviewer must approve the complete A2 book.
- Real learner evidence must test outcomes and usability; automated tests cannot replace it.
- B1 still requires full expansion before the complete A1-to-B1 course claim can pass.
