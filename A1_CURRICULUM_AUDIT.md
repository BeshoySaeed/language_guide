# German A1 curriculum audit

## Decision

The internally reviewed A1 curriculum is structurally complete: 14 ordered chapters, 42 lessons, 1,000 vocabulary/form cards, 300 reusable sentence cards, 42 grammar topics, and 336 lesson questions. It now starts with German sound–spelling and sentence structure, then builds through practical situations into consolidation and production.

A learner is not declared A1-ready merely because this content exists. The app recommends A2 only when that learner has completed every A1 lesson, scored at least 80% on every lesson quiz and the final overall, reached at least 70% in each of seven assessed skills, and cleared due review. Native-speaker approval and a real learner pilot are still required before a public curriculum-quality claim.

## Current strengths

- Practical dialogues and natural repair phrases
- Greetings, personal details, food purchases, home and family, routines, groceries, transport, appointments, health, weather, and leisure
- Early coverage of verb-second order, questions, articles, possessives, modal verbs, separable verbs, and time expressions
- Listening playback, lesson quizzes, mixed practice, saved vocabulary, spaced review, and progress tracking
- Stable content IDs, answer validation, prerequisites, and offline lesson support

## Audit findings and corrections

| Area | Finding | Required correction |
|---|---|---|
| Order | Sounds and sentence structure came after situational lessons | Inserted “Sound and structure” as Chapter 1 |
| Pronunciation | No systematic alphabet, umlaut, ch, sch, z, w, vowel-length, or stress progression | Foundation lessons added; later lessons must recycle these contrasts |
| Grammar | Negation, present forms, cases, pronouns, imperatives, connectors, location/movement, and past-time narration needed a coherent sequence | Added sequenced coverage and a three-lesson consolidation chapter |
| Vocabulary | Coverage was far below the 1,000-card release target | Added a balanced high-frequency bank of nouns, verbs and forms, adjectives, connectors, numbers, dates, and social phrases; cards remain attached to relevant lessons and review |
| Sentences | Coverage was below the 300-sentence release target | Expanded every final chapter with reusable sentence frames, reaching 300 authored cards |
| Topics | Work, study, dates, identity, home, services, shopping, restaurant, travel, accommodation, communication, and emergencies were incomplete | Added complete real-life chapters in the dependency order below |
| Writing | No controlled or short free-writing task | Added typed final-production prompts and a dedicated message-writing lesson |
| Speaking | Learners repeated audio but were not assessed | Added microphone-supported speaking prompts with typed fallback and a dedicated speaking-strategy lesson |
| Mastery | Lesson completion alone was not a reliable A1 exit | Added a persisted readiness calculation using lesson mastery, seven skill scores, and due review |
| Final test | The checkpoint contained 15 recognition questions in five skills | Expanded A1 to 21 questions across seven skills with an 80% overall and 70% per-skill floor |

## Target teaching sequence

1. Sound and structure — alphabet, spelling, sound map, verb-second foundations
2. Everyday essentials — greetings, bakery, café
3. My life and my day — personal details, family/home, routine
4. Out and about — groceries, transport, appointments
5. Wellbeing and plans — health, weather/clothing, leisure
6. Calendar and identity — dates, countries/languages, forms
7. Work and study — classroom, workplace, schedules
8. Home and neighborhood — rooms/furniture, locations, city services
9. Food and shopping — restaurant, quantities, clothes/sizes
10. Travel basics — directions, tickets, accommodation
11. Communication and safety — phone/messages, requests, emergencies
12. Grammar consolidation — negation, cases/pronouns, connectors and word order
13. A1 bridge — weather/reasons, social plans, descriptions/comparisons
14. A1 review and production — practical reading, guided writing, guided speaking

## A1 exit standard

- All required A1 lessons completed with lesson quizzes at or above 80%
- At least 1,000 useful vocabulary cards and 300 useful sentence cards in the reviewed curriculum
- Final assessment at or above 80% overall
- Vocabulary, sentences, grammar, reading, listening, writing, and speaking each at or above 70%
- Previously weak objectives reassigned automatically until their follow-up checks pass
- No overdue foundational review backlog at the time A2 is recommended
- Full-book native-speaker review recorded before public release

Automated validation proves structure, coverage, stable IDs, and deterministic scoring. It cannot prove pronunciation quality across every browser voice, native naturalness, or real learner outcomes; those remain explicit human validation gates.
