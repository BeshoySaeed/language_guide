# German native-review checklist

Review each book in full before adding native-review approval to its provenance.

- German is idiomatic and appropriate for the stated CEFR level.
- English translations preserve the intended meaning.
- Articles, gender, plurals, cases, conjugation, and word order are correct.
- Pronunciation hints do not encourage materially wrong pronunciation.
- Dialogues sound natural for the setting and register.
- Formal and informal address are used consistently.
- Questions have one unambiguously correct answer and useful explanations.
- Cultural and practical claims are accurate and not misleading.
- Speech playback text contains only the intended German utterance.
- Any requested corrections were applied and checked again.

Record the reviewer’s real name, locale, review date, full-book scope, and approval only after all blocking findings are closed.
