# A1 learner-pilot protocol

## Purpose

Test whether real beginners can use the A1 course, retain its content, and reach the measured A2-readiness gate without a release-blocking learning or product failure.

## Participants

- Recruit at least 10 consenting adult learners.
- Learners may know isolated German words, but must not already hold an A1 certificate or demonstrate A2 ability.
- Use anonymous participant IDs in the repository. Keep consent records and personal data outside the repository.
- Record accessibility needs and device/browser categories without personally identifying details.

## Required journey

Each participant must register, complete all 42 A1 lessons, use vocabulary and phrase audio, complete due reviews, repeat quizzes below 80%, take the seven-skill final, verify progress in a second session, and reach the in-app readiness decision or record why they could not.

## Per-participant evidence

- entry screening result and device/browser category;
- lessons completed and quizzes at 80% or higher;
- due reviews remaining;
- final overall and seven skill scores;
- readiness result and task observations;
- issue IDs encountered;
- withdrawal or non-completion reason.

## Pass criteria

The pilot can be marked passed only when:

- at least 10 eligible participants started and every recorded participant completed the journey;
- every participant has a complete result record;
- at least 80% reach the A2-readiness decision without answer-changing intervention;
- all critical and high findings are closed and rechecked;
- speech, authentication, progress, review, mobile, accessibility, and final-assessment journeys have coverage;
- the pilot lead signs and dates the report.

Use "quality/a1-learner-pilot-results.example.json". The audit deliberately treats a missing real results file as pending.
